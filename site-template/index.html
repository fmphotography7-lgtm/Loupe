About the Artist

Twenty-five years of noticing what others walk past.

For more than 25 years, photography has been both my passion and my creative outlet. Inspired by the rugged beauty of the West Coast, I capture everything from wildlife and landscapes to architecture, weddings, special events, portraits, real estate, product and dance photography.

My approach is relaxed, fun and collaborative. That is what lets me make authentic images while bringing each client's vision to life.

Whether it is a breathtaking landscape, a family milestone, or a once-in-a-lifetime celebration, the goal is simple: beautiful, lasting images that preserve life's most meaningful moments.
